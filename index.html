The Project

A planned community at Islamabad’s green edge.

Margalla Enclave has been publicly described as a joint CDA-DHA development in Islamabad. The project vision combines residential neighborhoods, commercial areas, landscaped spaces and modern urban infrastructure.

This website would focus on making project information easier to understand, while directing visitors to official CDA/DHA sources for authoritative approvals, allotment and transaction-related matters.
